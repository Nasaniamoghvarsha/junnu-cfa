## EXAM STRATEGY CHEAT SHEET

### Time Management
- 180 questions, 270 minutes total
- **90 seconds per question**
- Flag and skip questions taking >2 minutes
- Return to flagged questions at end of session

### Question Approach
1. Read the LAST sentence first (what are they asking?)
2. Read the full stem
3. Identify the concept/Learning Outcome
4. Predict the answer before looking at options
5. Eliminate wrong answers
6. Select and move on

### High-Value Reminders
- "LEAST likely" = find the FALSE/INCORRECT statement
- CFA FX: EUR/USD = 1.10 means 1 EUR = 1.10 USD
- NPV always beats IRR for mutually exclusive projects
- D₁ not D₀ in Gordon Growth Model
- Price and yield move in OPPOSITE directions
- WACC uses MARKET values, not book values
- Callable bonds: OAS < Z-spread

### Ethics Quick Checklist
- Strictest law/standard applies
- Disclosure ≠ prohibition
- Fair ≠ equal
- Mosaic theory = public + non-material nonpublic OK
- Client trades before personal trades (always)
- GIPS = firm-wide, only firms managing assets
