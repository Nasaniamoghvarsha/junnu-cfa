# CFA Level I — Mock Exam System Blueprint

This document outlines the design structure, topic distribution, difficulty mix, and scoring methodology of the CFA Level I Mock Examinations.

---

## 🎯 1. THE SIMULATION FRAMEWORK

Each mock exam is structured to replicate the official CFA Institute computer-based testing (CBT) environment. 

* **Total Questions:** 180 multiple-choice questions (3 options: A, B, C)
* **Total Time:** 4.5 hours (270 minutes) of active testing
* **Pacing Target:** 90 seconds per question

### Session Breakdown
The mock exam is divided into two distinct sessions, matching the official curriculum split:

* **Session 1 (Morning Paper):** 90 questions | 135 minutes  
  *Covers: Ethical & Professional Standards, Quantitative Methods, Economics, and Financial Statement Analysis.*
* **Session 2 (Afternoon Paper):** 90 questions | 135 minutes  
  *Covers: Corporate Issuers, Equity Investments, Fixed Income, Derivatives, Alternative Investments, and Portfolio Management.*

## 📈 3. DIFFICULTY & QUESTION TYPE MIX

To ensure realistic testing, each mock exam contains a carefully curated mix of difficulties and styles:

### Difficulty Level Distribution
1. **Level 1 (Foundation):** 10% (~18 Qs) — Recall of terms, direct definition checks.
2. **Level 2 (Basic):** 20% (~36 Qs) — Standard single-step calculation or simple concept pairing.
3. **Level 3 (Standard):** 40% (~72 Qs) — Typical CFA-level questions requiring multi-step reasoning or distinguishing between close options.
4. **Level 4 (Difficult):** 20% (~36 Qs) — Complex multi-step calculations, subtle ethical scenarios, or tricky variable changes.
5. **Level 5 (Very Difficult):** 10% (~18 Qs) — Advanced synthesis, outlier conceptual adjustments (e.g. lease adjustments under specific scenarios).

### Question Type Distribution
* **Conceptual (30%):** Tests understanding of theories, relationships, and classifications.
* **Calculation (40%):** Involves mathematical computation using formulas and the financial calculator.
* **Application / Scenario (30%):** Short cases or situational vignettes where standards, policies, or decisions must be evaluated.

## 🛡️ 4. HOW TO SIMULATE EXAM DAY

To get the most out of your mock exams, replicate the official exam conditions perfectly:

1. **Calculate the timing:** Block out a 4.5-hour period. Set a timer for 135 minutes for each session.
2. **Remove all study aids:** No formula sheets, no books, and no internet access.
3. **Use only approved tools:** Use only your TI BA II Plus or HP 12C calculator. Use dry-erase sheets or plain scratch paper for calculations.
4. **Take the scheduled break:** Take a 30-minute break between Session 1 and Session 2. Eat a light snack, hydrate, and stretch. Do not check your answers or review notes during the break.
5. **No guessing reviews:** When taking the mock, flag questions where you guessed. Review these even if you get them right.

## 📊 5. SCORE ANALYSIS & REMEDIATION PROTOCOL

Once you complete a mock exam, evaluate your results systematically. Do not just look at the percentage score.

### Score Interpretation
* **75%+ (🟢 Strong):** On track for a comfortable pass. Shift focus to maintaining recall and refining pacing.
* **65% - 74% (🟡 Borderline):** Typical pass boundary. Identify the 2 weakest subjects and run targeted drills on them.
* **Below 65% (🔴 At Risk):** Underpass threat. Re-review the core formula bank, error bank, and do a complete audit of incorrect answers.

### Post-Mock Remediation Steps

1. **Analyze by Subject:** Identify any subject where you scored below 70%.
2. **Identify Error Patterns:** Fill out your [Personal Error Bank](../dashboard/error-bank-system.md) for every incorrect question.
3. **Formulate the Correction Action:**
   * If it was a *calculation error* → run 10 [Formula Drills](../formula-bank/formula-drills.md) on that specific formula.
   * If it was a *conceptual trap* → read the corresponding section in the [Common Trap Catalog](../pattern-library/trap-catalog.md).
   * If it was a *reading error* → run through the [Question Construction Recipes](../pattern-library/question-construction-recipes.md) to practice isolating stems from distractors.
