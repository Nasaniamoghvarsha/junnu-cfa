### Q-MOCK-0001 | Difficulty: 3 | Time: 90s | Pattern: Ethics Scenario | Trap: Strictest Law Application

**Question:**
Maria Chen, CFA, works as a senior investment analyst in Country A, where local securities law permits analysts to accept personal gifts up to USD 500 from corporate executives without disclosure. However, Chen's employer imposes a global policy adhering strictly to the CFA Institute Code and Standards. A company Chen covers sends her a luxury watch valued at USD 350 after she publishes a research report. Which of the following actions is most appropriate for Chen?

A) Accept the gift since it is under Country A's legal limit of USD 500
B) Refuse the gift or surrender it to her firm because the stricter CFA Standard applies
C) Keep the gift but disclose it to her research supervisor within 30 days

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Under Standard I(A) Knowledge of the Law, members must comply with the stricter of applicable laws, rules, or the CFA Institute Code and Standards. Standard I(B) Independence and Objectivity requires analysts to refuse gifts that could reasonably be expected to compromise their independence or objectivity. A USD 350 luxury watch is not token; therefore, the stricter CFA Standard prevails over Country A's permissive law.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- A: Incorrect — members cannot follow permissive local law when the CFA Code and Standards impose a stricter requirement.
- C: Incorrect — post-hoc disclosure does not cure the acceptance of a substantial gift that threatens objectivity.

**LO Reference:** ETH-02-01-LO01 (Standard I(A) Knowledge of the Law)

### Q-MOCK-0002 | Difficulty: 3 | Time: 90s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Compounding Frequency

**Question:**
An investor deposits USD 10,000 into a savings account offering a nominal annual interest rate of 6.0%, compounded quarterly. What is the total value of the investment at the end of 5 years?

A) USD 13,000.00
B) USD 13,468.55
C) USD 13,488.50

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Quarterly interest rate $r = 6.0\% / 4 = 1.5\% = 0.015$. Number of compounding periods $n = 5 \times 4 = 20$.
Future value formula: $FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n = 10,000 \times (1.015)^{20} = 10,000 \times 1.346855 = \text{USD } 13,468.55$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- A: Incorrect — uses simple interest without compounding ($\text{USD } 10,000 + 10,000 \times 0.06 \times 5 = \text{USD } 13,000$).
- C: Incorrect — uses monthly compounding periods ($n = 60$) at $0.5\%$ rate.

**LO Reference:** QUANT-01-01-LO02 (Time Value of Money Compounding)

### Q-MOCK-0003 | Difficulty: 3 | Time: 90s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Preferred Dividend Deduction

**Question:**
Alpha Corporation reports net income of USD 5,000,000 and has 1,000,000 weighted average common shares outstanding. Alpha has 100,000 cumulative preferred shares paying a dividend of USD 5.00 per share. What is Alpha's Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS)?

A) USD 4.50
B) USD 5.00
C) USD 5.50

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Basic EPS $= (\text{Net Income} - \text{Preferred Dividends}) / \text{Weighted Average Common Shares}$.
Preferred Dividends $= 100,000 \times \text{USD } 5.00 = \text{USD } 500,000$.
Basic EPS $= (\text{USD } 5,000,000 - \text{USD } 500,000) / 1,000,000 = \text{USD } 4.50$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- B: Incorrect — fails to deduct preferred dividends from net income.
- C: Incorrect — erroneously adds preferred dividends to net income.

**LO Reference:** FSA-03-01-LO02 (Basic Earnings Per Share Calculation)

### Q-MOCK-0004 | Difficulty: 3 | Time: 90s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Gordon Growth DDM

**Question:**
An analyst calculates that a stock will pay an annual dividend of USD 2.50 next year ($D_1$). The required rate of return on equity is 10.0%, and dividends are expected to grow at a constant rate of 4.0% indefinitely. According to the Gordon Growth Model, what is the intrinsic value of the stock?

A) USD 41.67
B) USD 62.50
C) USD 65.00

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Gordon Growth Model: $V_0 = D_1 / (r - g)$.
Given $D_1 = \text{USD } 2.50$, $r = 10.0\% = 0.10$, and $g = 4.0\% = 0.04$.
$V_0 = 2.50 / (0.10 - 0.04) = 2.50 / 0.06 = \text{USD } 62.50$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- A: Incorrect — erroneously adds $g$ to $r$ in denominator.
- C: Incorrect — compounds $D_1$ again by $(1 + g)$ in numerator.

**LO Reference:** EQ-03-01-LO02 (Gordon Growth Model Valuation)
