### Q-MOCK1-ETH-001 | Difficulty: 2 | Time: 60s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Curriculum Trap

**Question:**
During a portfolio review, Analyst #1 receives nonpublic information regarding a regulatory penalty. According to Standard I(A) Knowledge of the Law and Standard II(A), what is the required compliance protocol?
A) Act on the information immediately before market open.
B) Refrain from trading or inducing others to trade until the information is publicly disclosed.
C) Inform existing high-net-worth clients verbally while withholding written research reports.


**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Standard II(A) strictly mandates that members in possession of material nonpublic information must not trade or induce others to trade until public dissemination occurs.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- Option A: Incorrect — trading on material nonpublic information violates Standard II(A).
- Option C: Incorrect — selectively disclosing to favored clients violates both Standard II(A) and Standard III(B) Fair Dealing.

**LO Reference:** ETH-LO-01

### Q-MOCK1-QUANT-028 | Difficulty: 2 | Time: 60s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Curriculum Trap

**Question:**
An investor places USD 10,000 into a corporate bond savings fund offering a nominal annual rate of 5.0% compounded quarterly. What is the total investment value at the end of 5 years (20 quarters)?
A) USD 12,500.00
B) USD 12,820.37
C) USD 13,076.78


**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Future Value formula: $FV = PV \times (1 + r/4)^{20} = 10000 \times (1 + 0.0125)^{20} = \text{USD } 12,820.37$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- Option A: Incorrect — this incorrectly uses simple uncompounded interest (USD 12,500.00).
- Option C: Incorrect — contains compounding period frequency error.

**LO Reference:** QUANT-LO-01
