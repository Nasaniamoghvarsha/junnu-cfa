### Q-MOCK1-EQ-001 | Difficulty: 2 | Time: 60s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Curriculum Trap

**Question:**
An equity analyst estimates next year's annual dividend ($D_1$) for a firm will be USD 2.00. The required return on equity is 10.0% and constant dividend growth is 4.0%. According to the Gordon Growth Model, what is the intrinsic value per share?

A) USD 14.29
B) USD 33.33
C) USD 34.67

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Gordon Growth Model: $V_0 = D_1 / (r - g) = \text{USD } 2.00 / (0.10 - 0.04) = \text{USD } 33.33$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- Option A: Incorrect — adds growth rate instead of subtracting in denominator.
- Option C: Incorrect — multiplies $D_1$ by $(1+g)$ again; $(1+g)$ multiplier is applied only when given $D_0$.

**LO Reference:** EQ-LO-01

### Q-MOCK1-FI-023 | Difficulty: 2 | Time: 60s | Pattern: Calculation | Trap: Curriculum Trap

**Question:**
A corporate bond has a flat (clean) market price of USD 1,020.00. The accrued interest since the last coupon date is USD 25.00. What is the full (dirty) price of the bond?

A) USD 1,020.00
B) USD 1,045.00
C) USD 1,070.00

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Full (Dirty) Price $= \text{Flat Price} + \text{Accrued Interest} = \text{USD } 1,020.00 + \text{USD } 25.00 = \text{USD } 1,045.00$.

**Wrong Answer Analysis:**
- Option A: Incorrect — gives flat price without adding accrued interest.
- Option C: Incorrect — adds double annual coupon interest.

**LO Reference:** FI-LO-01
