## 3. READINESS SCORING FORMULA

Your overall readiness score is **weighted by exam weight**, not by question count.

```
Readiness Score = Σ (Subject Accuracy × Subject Exam Weight)

Example:
  Ethics:  88% × 17.5% weight  = 15.4
  Quant:   82% × 7.5%          = 6.2
  Econ:    75% × 7.5%          = 5.6
  FSA:     90% × 12.5%         = 11.3
  ...
  ────────────────────────────────
  Weighted Readiness Score     = ___%
```

**Interpretation:**
| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 90%+ | 🟢 Exam Ready | Maintain + focus on time management |
| 80–89% | 🟡 Strong | Target weak subjects for final push |
| 70–79% | 🟠 Needs Work | Intensive review of weak areas |
| <70% | 🔴 At Risk | Restructure study plan immediately |

## 6. TIME MANAGEMENT TRACKER

CFA Level I timing: 90 questions in 135 minutes = 90 seconds per question.

Practice calibrating your pace:

| Session | Target | Actual | Over/Under | Action |
|---------|--------|--------|------------|--------|
| Mock 1 AM | 135 min | ___ min | ___ min | _______ |
| Mock 1 PM | 135 min | ___ min | ___ min | _______ |

**Warning Signs:**
- Spending >3 min on any one question → flag and move on
- Completing <70 questions in 135 min → critical pacing issue
- Feeling "stuck" for >2 questions in a row → skip, come back

**Strategy:**
```
First pass:  Answer what you know confidently  (aim for 70–75 questions)
Second pass: Return to flagged questions        (remaining time)
Guess rule:  If <30 seconds left, guess B (statistically ~33% hit rate)
```

## 7. RETENTION CHECK SYSTEM

The retention check is the most important — and most often skipped — part of preparation.

**How it works:**
1. After studying a topic, mark the date.
2. Exactly 7 days later, test yourself on 5 questions from that topic WITHOUT reviewing first.
3. Score it. If <80%, the material is NOT retained. Go back.
4. Exactly 14 days later, test again with 5 different questions.
5. If 80%+ at both intervals, that topic is retained.
